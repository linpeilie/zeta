# 行为准则 / Code of Conduct

## 我们的承诺

为了营造开放友好的环境，我们承诺让每个参与本项目的人都不因年龄、体型、身心障碍、族群、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育背景、社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教信仰或性取向而遭受骚扰。

我们承诺以开放、包容、多元和健康的方式互动。

## 我们的准则

有助于营造良好环境的行为：

- 对他人表达同理与善意
- 尊重不同的观点和经验
- 得体地给出并接受建设性反馈
- 承担责任，向受影响者道歉，并从中学习
- 关注对整个社区最有利的做法，而不只是个人偏好

不可接受的行为：

- 使用带有性意味的言语或图像，以及任何形式的性关注或性骚扰
- 挑衅、辱骂或贬损性言论，以及人身或政治攻击
- 公开或私下的骚扰
- 未经明确许可公开他人的私人信息，例如实际住址或电子邮箱
- 其他在专业场合中可以合理认定为不当的行为

## 技术分歧不等于冒犯

本项目在架构上有一批相当严格的约束。**评审时对代码提出明确、直接的意见是正常且必要的**，被要求修改也不意味着对你个人的否定。

区别在于：对事——「这个改动在共享层引入了 Provider 分支，请挪到 adapter 里」；对人——针对提交者本身的贬低。前者欢迎，后者不接受。

## 适用范围

本准则适用于所有项目空间（Issue、Pull Request、Discussions、提交信息、代码注释），以及在公开场合代表本项目时的行为。

## 举报

如遇滥用、骚扰或其他不可接受的行为，请通过 GitHub 私信联系维护者 [@linpeilie](https://github.com/linpeilie)，或使用仓库的私密上报渠道。

所有投诉都会被审阅和调查，并给出必要且恰当的回应。维护者有义务为举报人保密。

维护者有权移除、编辑或拒绝任何不符合本准则的评论、提交、代码、Issue 及其他贡献，并在必要时对其认为不当、威胁、冒犯或有害的行为采取临时或永久封禁。

## 出处

本准则改编自 [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org) 2.1 版，并针对本项目的技术评审文化补充了「技术分歧不等于冒犯」一节。

# Code of Conduct (English)

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologizing to those affected, and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole, not just personal preference

Unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Technical disagreement is not hostility

This project enforces a fairly strict set of architectural constraints. **Clear, direct feedback during review is normal and necessary**, and being asked to change something is not a judgment about you.

The line is between critiquing work — "this change introduces provider branching in a shared layer; please move it into the adapter" — and disparaging the person who submitted it. The former is welcome; the latter is not.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages, code comments) and when an individual is representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer [@linpeilie](https://github.com/linpeilie) via GitHub, or through the repository's private reporting channel.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly, and will result in a response deemed necessary and appropriate to the circumstances. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and to temporarily or permanently ban any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
